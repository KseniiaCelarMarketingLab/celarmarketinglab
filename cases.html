From dense R&D to positioning farmers understand

BioCycle Labs · Cleantech and agri-innovation, Netherlands
3plain-language outcomes the new website is built around
4workstreams: research, partnerships, SEO, LinkedIn
1partnership pipeline built from research

The new homepage

Positioning in plain language

Product pages

Each technology explained by what it does for the buyer

The task

BioCycle Labs builds Black Soldier Fly and modular waste-treatment technology for European farms. Their value proposition was technical and research-heavy, and farm operators could not act on it.

What we did

Researched EU sustainability trends, waste-to-resource technology and competitors
Mapped partners across research institutions, industry and technology providers
Rewrote the homepage and core pages around lower emissions, better waste processing and valuable by-products
Built an SEO page structure and a series of LinkedIn articles
What we learned

In deep tech, turning research into language a buyer can act on is the main marketing task.

Content examples

Three feeds, one visual system

Instagram grids with views per post

The task

Three Instagram accounts for the Netherlands, France and Europe were posting about 29 times a month each, yet over 90 days they gained a combined net of −4 followers. The brand needed one voice across Dutch, French and international audiences, without the content reading like a translation.

What we did

One master content calendar, adapted per market
Tone-of-voice guide for Dutch and French audiences
One visual system across all three accounts
Carousels for stories, Reels for product and behind the scenes
What we learned

One brand core scales across markets better than three separate strategies, as long as the tone-of-voice guide handles the local differences.

One content plan for a team and its head coach

XcelleRIDE · International show jumping team, UAE
1.18Maccounts reached in March 2024, team and coach
82,199team account reach in March 2024
46,582accounts engaged in March 2024

Two accounts, one plan

Team account and head coach’s personal account

Results, March 2024

Native Instagram Insights

The task

An international show jumping team based in the UAE wanted to promote its brand through the team account and the head coach’s personal Instagram. Goals: attract a new audience, develop the team and coach brands, attract sponsors and build loyalty.

What we did

Content around team life and behind-the-scenes access
Merchandise stories tied to the team identity
Tested engagement formats over a two-month sprint
Kept the plan running through Ramadan in April, when the team’s schedule changed and figures dipped
What we learned

One tested plan for both accounts built more recognition than two separate strategies would have.

The concept book

Equitrip, 2025

The task

Choose a visual style and tone of voice for Instagram before starting regular content.

What we did

Three concepts, each with colours, fonts and design elements
Each tested on horse profiles, results posts and rider spotlights
Presented together in one book with the team’s real horses and riders
What we learned

Comparing directions on the same real posts helps a client choose on substance, not on a mood board.
