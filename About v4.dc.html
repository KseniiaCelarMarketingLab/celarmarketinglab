A marketing studio from the Netherlands

Celar helps brands replace scattered marketing activity with one clear plan, and then carries it out. Every project is led by the founder, with specialist partners for design, video, ads and local-language copy.

Founder

Kseniia Kalashnikova is a marketing strategist based in Utrecht with 7+ years of practice. She moved to the Netherlands on her own and built her career across international education, freelance work and entrepreneurship before founding Celar.

She knows both sides of marketing: the strategy, and the founder’s reality, where one person makes the decisions, creates the content and finds the clients. That is why Celar’s advice is built to be clear, practical and realistic for small teams.
